# Design — glimmer-concurrency-ceiling

## Approach

- Declare, don't re-plumb: `ignition/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/recipe.py:68` becomes `concurrency=8, max_concurrency=16` — the DeepSeek-style default-below-ceiling shape (`ignition/recipes/deepseek41/recipe.py:64`, 32/64): an unflagged export prepares the eight requests the operator runs (batches 1/2/4/8, 9 state rows, 128 sampled rows), `--max-concurrency 16` prepares the ceiling, and the console always passes `--max-concurrency <max_num_seqs>` (`~/LocalApps/vllm-env/console/launchers/docker.py:908`), so its profile's 16 selects the ceiling package; every consumer already reads the declaration (`ignition/planning.py:540-541` admission bound, `ignition/components/capacity.py:60-62` batches 1/2/4/8/16, `helios/src/execution/manifest.rs:435-438` 1..64, `ignition/cli/serve.py:68` queue and workers, `ignition/cli/help.py:33` the printed range).
- The packed catalog becomes `Speculation(draft_tokens=15, token_capacity=128, packed_verification=True, graph_budget=64)`: `parallel_speculative_shapes(16, 1..16, 128, 64)` gives 64 packed rounds (10/13/16/16/9 across batches 1/2/4/8/16; batch-8 rows 9–128, batch-16 rows 17–128) plus 5 width-one rounds at the ceiling, and 64 (13/16/18/17 across 1/2/4/8) plus 4 at the default, and its 10 endpoint shapes pass `derive`'s budget check (`ignition/planning.py:449-456`). 128 rows keep the full 15-draft depth through the operator's expected C8 and 7 drafts at 16; the served acceptance-by-depth counters (`~/models/.cache/glimmer-1m-passkey-raw/grid/100000-0.1/metrics-after.txt`, 361 width-16 rounds) put 18% of accepted tokens at depths 8–15, which 64 rows would drop at C8, and only that same 18% at C16 for 256 rows, a load the operator does not expect; on the single 96 GB RTX PRO 6000 the doubled speculative scratch (`decode_tokens` 64 → 128) is small against ~55 GiB of KV pages. `--speculative-token-capacity` at export still overrides it.
- `PreparationConfig.max_active` keeps its construction default of 4 (`ignition/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/bootstrap.py:68`): `ignition/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/recipe.py:35` passes `plan.concurrency` on every export path, so the default only serves direct construction in tests; the catalog test gains a `max_active=16` case instead of moving the default.
- `_GRAPH_BYTES` (`ignition/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/recipe.py:56-57`) is a measured inventory, so it is re-measured from the concurrency-16 export's printed VRAM inventory (`ignition/preparation/runtime_package.py:238-240`) and its comment names the new export entry; the plan report uses it for the fit check and the page estimate, Helios sizes pages from actual free memory at serve time.
- Memory at 16 on the 96 GB GB202: 17 permanently backed state rows × 62,914,560 B = 1.0 GiB (`ignition/recipes/declaration.py:303-307`); graphs grow by the batch-8/16 decode, resume, restore and snapshot variants and five packed rounds; KV pages fall by the same amount but stay far above the 8,192 pages one 1,048,576-token request needs, so `context = min(declared, pages × 128)` (`ignition/planning.py:336`) stays 1M while `guaranteed_tokens` (`:340`) becomes pages ÷ 16 × 128 — documented as the admission-level limit.
- Pins move with the number: `tests/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/test_config.py:99` (4 → 8); `tests/recipes/muse_glimmer_nvfp4/test_preparation.py:45-62` gains a default-8 and a ceiling-16 catalog case (`(8, 16, 128)`/`(8, 16, 9)`; `(16, 16, 128)`/`(16, 16, 17)`; packed rounds ≤ 64; sampled rows 128 and 256).
- Docs: `docs/model-preparation.md:1110` "four concurrent requests" → "eight concurrent requests by default and up to sixteen with `--max-concurrency 16` (a lone request may reach the full 1,048,576 tokens; with every admitted request active each is guaranteed pages ÷ concurrency × 128)"; `:1134` "batches one, two, and four" → "batches one, two, four, and eight (one through sixteen at `--max-concurrency 16`)".
- The served check at 16 cannot use the frozen speculation workload: `qualify/speculative_workload.py:31,288` caps `code_edit` at 8 cases per stage and `request_groups` at concurrency 8 by design (its execution plan is frozen), so the check is sixteen simultaneous `curl` streams of one deterministic prompt with `/metrics` read before and after (`flare_requests_admitted_total`, `flare_speculative_rounds_total{batch="16",width="16"}`, `flare_speculative_accepted_drafts_total`); `qualify/speculative_benchmark.py` and the sweep stay untouched.
- GPU legs run through the console only (owner's rule; the agent never stops the owner's instance): once GPU 0 is free, build `local/flare-ignition:<sha8>` from the committed ref, duplicate the "2026-11-07 Flare Muse Glimmer 1M" profile onto that image with `max_num_seqs` 16 (the console passes it as `--max-concurrency 16`, so the export measures the ceiling), start it, read the export log (plan line `concurrency 16`, VRAM inventory, `Allocate cache` pages and snapshot slots), then fire sixteen streams at once and record status codes, wall times and the metric deltas under `qualify/results/glimmer-c16/summary.json`; a lower accepted-drafts-per-round figure than batch 4's 3.5 is a measurement, not a blocker.
- The `muse-glimmer-serving` spec's "Declared capacities and costs match the prepared layout" requirement moves to concurrency 16 and gains a scenario for the 16-request catalog and one for admission at 16.
